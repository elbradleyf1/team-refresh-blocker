# Privacy policy

**Team Refresh Blocker for F1 Fantasy**

Last updated: 6 October 2026

## Summary

This extension does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data or user data.

## What the extension does with data

- **No data collection.** The extension does not collect any information about you, your browsing, your F1 Fantasy account or your team.
- **No transmission.** The extension does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It has no servers, analytics or tracking.
- **Local setting only.** The only thing stored is whether you have switched blocking on or off. This is kept locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage and never leaves your device.
- **Limited scope.** The extension only runs on `fantasy.formula1.com`. On that site, it checks the web address of the site's own requests, inside your browser, to recognise background team downloads and block them. These addresses are not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
- **No remote code.** All of the extension's code is included in the extension itself. It does not download or run code from the internet.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
